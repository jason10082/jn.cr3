Do you charge for travel?

Standard rates include travel within Penang Island and immediate mainland areas. For outstation events, a flat travel allowance applies to cover petrol and tolls:

  • Up to 40km (Penang Island & Mainland): No extra charge
  • 41km to 80km: RM 50 flat rate
  • Beyond 80km: Custom quote provided upon inquiry

Do you shoot weddings as the main photographer?

Thank you so much for the interest! At this time, I am strictly focusing on smaller private events, corporate functions, and working as a second shooter alongside established wedding studios. I am not taking on solo, main-photographer wedding bookings. However, if you have any pre-wedding events, engagements, or private dinners coming up, I would love to be a part of them!
