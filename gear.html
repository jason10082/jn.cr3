The Kit

A complete breakdown of the cameras, lenses, and accessories I use to bring my creative vision to life.

Cameras

Canon

EOS R6 Mark II

My primary hybrid powerhouse. Blazing fast autofocus and incredible low-light performance for both high-res photos and 4K video.

Canon

EOS R50

The perfect compact b-cam. Lightweight, versatile, and excellent for behind-the-scenes content, travel vlogging, and tight setups.

Lenses

Canon RF

28-70mm f/2.8 IS STM

My go-to standard zoom. A fast constant aperture combined with a versatile focal range makes this perfect for everyday shooting and portraits.

Canon EF

70-200mm f/2.8L IS II USM

The legendary workhorse telephoto. Unmatched sharpness, beautiful background compression, and rugged durability for events and sports.

Canon EF

50mm f/1.8 STM

The classic "nifty fifty". An absolutely essential, lightweight prime lens that delivers beautiful bokeh and razor-sharp images.

Canon EF

16-35mm f/4L IS USM

An ultra-wide L-series zoom delivering exceptional sharpness and image stabilization. Perfect for expansive landscapes, tight event venues, and architectural shots.

Support & Audio

DJI

RS 4 Gimbal

Next-generation stabilization. Delivers buttery smooth cinematic video footage with upgraded motor strength and quick vertical shooting capabilities.

Ulanzi

TT31 Travel Tripod

A highly versatile and portable tripod that doubles as a quick light stand or grip for creators constantly on the move.

Sennheiser

MKE 200 Mic

A compact, directional on-camera microphone that dramatically upgrades audio quality while effectively rejecting wind and handling noise.

Lighting & Accessories

Godox

TT685II Speedlite

A reliable, high-power flash with TTL and High-Speed Sync. Essential for controlling lighting at events or overpowering the sun outdoors.

Haida

ND & CPL Filters

Essential glass for cutting glare and controlling light intake. Perfect for shooting wide-open in bright sunlight or capturing long exposures.

Bags & Carry

PGYTECH

OneGo 22L Backpack

A premium, modular backpack offering rugged protection, weather resistance, and stylish organization for transporting the entire primary kit.

K&F Concept

Alpha Air 25L

A sleek, lightweight camera backpack designed for quick side access and comfortable all-day carry when navigating the city.

K&F Concept

Sling Bag 10L (Camouflage)

A rugged, compact 10L sling bag featuring a stealthy camouflage design. Perfect for light, run-and-gun shooting with quick, cross-body access to essential gear.
